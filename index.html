0-1 Support

The progressive overload tracker. 0-1 tells you the exact weight and reps for every session, and next session’s numbers set themselves.

Need help? Email hi@noblood.io. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → 0-1 → Cancel. Do it at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open 0-1 → Profile (or the paywall) → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data or my account?

Open 0-1 → Profile → Delete data (erases your workouts, goals and settings) or Delete account (erases everything and removes your account). Account deletion needs an internet connection. You can also email us. See the Privacy Policy.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my workouts?

Link Sign in with Apple in Profile before switching. Then sign in on the new phone to restore your data.

Why did my target weight go down or stay the same?

0-1 adds weight when you hit the top of your rep range on every set. If you miss reps it repeats the weight, and after two tough sessions in a row it lowers it about 10% so you keep making progress. If you had a rough day, tap “Rough day? Don’t count it” on the result screen, or start an easy session next time.

Can I move my workout days?

Yes. Tap a training day in the week strip and choose Move. You can move it for this week only or every week.

Is 0-1 medical advice?

No. Talk to a doctor before starting an exercise program. See the Terms of Use.
