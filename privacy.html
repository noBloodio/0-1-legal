Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Last updated: October 5, 2026

The short version. 0-1 stores your workouts, goals and body details so it can tell you what weight and reps to do next. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your data or your whole account from inside the app at any time (Profile → Delete data / Delete account).

1. Who we are

0-1 (the “App”) is a progressive overload workout tracker operated by noBlood, LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at hi@noblood.io or by mail at 30 N. Gould St., Sheridan, WY 82801, USA.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Profile and body details: age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, training experience, preferred units (lb/kg), where you train (gym, home or calisthenics) and available equipment, and how much time you have per session.
  • Goals: up to three goals you choose (for example a target lift weight, a number of pull-ups, a muscle-up, building muscle or getting stronger) together with your current level and target.
  • Injuries or limitations (optional): anything you tell us about so that the plan avoids it. This can be health-related information. You can skip it, and you can remove it later in Profile.
  • Workout data: your plan and schedule (including days you move), sessions, exercises, sets, weights, reps, warm-up sets, easy or off days, cardio entries (type, minutes, distance and effort if you enter them), and the progression targets the App calculates from those entries.
  • Preferences: reminder time, unit settings, equipment increments and similar settings.
  • Messages to us: if you email support we receive your email address and whatever you write.

2.2 Account information

  • Anonymous account. When you first open the App we create an anonymous account for you so your data can be backed up and restored. It is identified by a random ID, not by your name or email.
  • Email sign-in (optional). If you choose to sign in with email, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are never stored in readable form; our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) stores only a salted hash. We use your email to sign you in, to send account messages such as sign-in confirmation or password reset, and to reply to support requests. We do not send marketing email.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose to link Sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple user identifier and, depending on your choice in Apple’s sign-in screen, your name and either your real email address or an Apple private relay address. We use this only to let you recover your account and data on a new device.

2.3 Subscription and purchase information

If you start a free trial or subscribe, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card or Apple ID password. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage entitlements; RevenueCat receives your anonymous app user ID, the product purchased, purchase and renewal dates, and subscription status.

2.4 Technical and usage information

Our service providers may automatically receive device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP address (we do not access precise location), time zone and diagnostic information such as crash reports. We do not currently use third-party product analytics or advertising tools in the App. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

2.5 Information we do not collect

  • We do not access Apple Health / HealthKit data.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and do not show third-party ads.

3. Health and fitness information

Body measurements, injuries and training history can be considered sensitive or health-related information in some places. We collect it only because you provide it, and only to build and adjust your training plan, show your progress, and keep your account working. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it. By entering it you consent to our processing it as described here. You can withdraw consent by deleting the information in the App (Profile) or by deleting your data or account. 0-1 does not provide medical advice (see our Terms of Use).

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar regions)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: contract (to provide the App you asked for); consent (for optional health-related details such as injuries, and for notifications, which you can withdraw at any time); legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, diagnostics and improving the App, balanced against your rights); and legal obligation (for example tax and accounting records for purchases).

6. Where your data lives

Your data is stored in a database on your device (SQLite). When syncing is on, a copy is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in a hosted database protected by row-level security so that each account can read and write only its own rows. The App works offline and syncs in the background when a connection is available. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and the backend encrypts data at rest.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that act on our instructions: Supabase (database and authentication) and RevenueCat (subscription management).
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, TestFlight and notifications on your device.
  • Professional advisers, authorities and courts where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • A successor if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets; we will tell you and give you a choice where required.
  • Anyone else only with your direction or consent.

8. Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. If you use Delete data, we erase your profile, goals, plan, workouts and progress from your device and from our backend. If you use Delete account, we also delete your account and sign-in identity. Backups are purged on a rolling basis, typically within 30 days. We may keep limited records longer where the law requires (for example purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat) or for security and dispute purposes. If you stop using the App without deleting your account, we may delete accounts that have been inactive for a long period after reasonable notice where possible.

9. Deleting your data and account

  • In the App: Profile → Delete data (erases your workouts, goals and settings but keeps your sign-in) or Delete account (erases everything and removes your account). Deleting your account needs an internet connection so we can remove the backend copy.
  • By email: write to hi@noblood.io from the email on your account or describe your anonymous account ID. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
